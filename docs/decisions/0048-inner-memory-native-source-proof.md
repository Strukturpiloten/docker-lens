## Strong controlled oracle and limits

One PID1 supervisor owns one bound96 MiB dd allocation child on every control.
Two barriers separate allocation from final supervisor exit. PID1 ignores the
release signal before its actual wait; interrupted wait cannot become child 137.
Readiness/publication helpers finish before exact two-member/one-member checks.
The supervisor survives until genuine identity-stable leaf-local max, oom and
exactly one oom_kill victim delta have been read, with observed oom.group = 0 and
zero effective swap. No protective writes are allowed; parent death, missing
live leaf, extra victim or uncertain state fails.

A local kill counter alone counts victims of any OOM killer and is insufficient
origin evidence. Local max/oom deltas supply the separate direct-pressure fact.
Positive controls complete the entire96 MiB record and retain no pressure/victim
increment. The actual same-CID Docker OOM event must be observed before releasing
PID1. The final genuine Engine OOMKilled flag remains mandatory; old Engine
ordering cannot be replaced by an invented flag or a relaxed success outcome.

PID1 may propagate child 137. Record that as supervisor-propagated child status,
never PID1 killed by OOM. Exec preserves child identity semantically; no direct
post-exec sample is claimed without observation. Private guest root `/` is valid:
bind its actual object/mount/namespaces and real limits/counters, independently
of PID1's current init.scope. Read the complete visible chain. Hidden ancestors
remain unobserved, never unbounded. Sampled visible headroom/local counters and
positive controls do not exclude every concurrent hidden/global disturbance or
prove unique causation for all events.
